GamePilot

Record. Detect. Score. Edit. Post.

GamePilot sits alongside every game you play. It optimizes your performance, auto-detects your best moments, scores them with local AI, and posts them to social with one tap. No cloud accounts. No subscriptions. Everything runs on your machine.

New in v3.0

Auto Clipper

Your gaming highlights, on autopilot. Play your session, and GamePilot does the rest: detection, scoring, editing, and posting.

Multi-Signal Detection

Six detection signals analyze your recordings in parallel: audio peaks, scene changes, speech transcription, YOLO game events, OCR filtering, and VLM scene understanding. Works with any game.

Local AI Scoring

Decima scores every clip candidate in 20ms on your CPU. Uncertain clips escalate to Qwen3 for deeper analysis and a written rationale explaining why the clip scored the way it did. Zero cloud, zero cost.

Mood Profiles

Score the same clip differently depending on your intent. Skill mode finds mechanical plays. Funny mode finds reactions. Montage mode finds rhythm. Viral mode finds hooks. Switch profiles in the review queue.

Three Editor Modes

Quick Post for one-tap publishing. Recipe Editor for template-based edits (TikTok Montage, YouTube Highlight, Skill Showcase, and more). Full Timeline for multi-track editing with effects and overlays.

Trending Content

Browse trending TikTok sounds, GIPHY stickers, and memes directly in the editor. Drag onto the timeline. Beat-sync your montage cuts to the music. Add voice mods, captions, and impact effects.

Auto-Post to 27 Platforms

Connected via Postiz: TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, X, Discord, Reddit, and more. Schedule 2-4 clips per day, and GamePilot handles formatting per platform automatically.

Platform

Intelligent optimization for every game

GamePilot runs passively in the background. Launch any game and it automatically starts working.

Auto Game Detection

Detects 14+ games automatically from running processes. Minecraft, League, Tarkov, RuneScape, Path of Exile, CS2, Fortnite, VALORANT, and more. Plus full Steam library scanning.

Real-Time Overlay

A transparent, always-on-top overlay shows performance metrics, resource hog warnings, and recommendations during gameplay.

Hardware Diagnostics

Profiles your CPU, RAM, GPU, and storage. Detects 20+ background processes that steal performance and warns you before launch.

Smart Recommendations

Rules-based optimization engine with safe, reversible actions. JVM tuning, performance mod suggestions, config optimization, all with preview, backup, and rollback.

Mod Store

Search, download, and install mods directly from Modrinth. One-click performance mod installation for Minecraft.

Session Tracking

Automatically tracks gaming sessions. Detects when a game starts and stops, records performance data, and generates post-session reports. Clip processing triggers on session end.

System Requirements

  • Windows 10 or later
  • 4 GB RAM minimum (8 GB for Clipper)
  • NVIDIA GPU recommended for detection (CPU fallback available)
  • FFmpeg (installed automatically or via winget)

Optional (for AI Scoring)

  • Ollama with Qwen3 for scoring rationale
  • NVIDIA GPU with 6+ GB VRAM for VLM scene analysis
